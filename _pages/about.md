I am a cognitive scientist and Assistant Professor in the [Department of Psychology and Counseling](https://www.astate.edu/) at **Arkansas State University**. I study the psychology of language: how we know what words mean and how those meanings are represented in the mind. I am also interested in how that knowledge shapes literacy, music, and social skills.

I earned my Ph.D. in Experimental Psychology (with a graduate certificate in Cognitive Science) from the University of Memphis, and my M.A. in Psychological Science from the University of North Carolina Wilmington.

Research interests
------
- Embodied cognition and language processing
- Semantic representation
- Reading and literacy skills
- Educational technology and intelligent tutoring systems
- Eye-tracking and mouse-tracking methods
